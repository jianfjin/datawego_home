EHR ETL Pipeline

A deterministic mapping pre-pass and Dagster-orchestrated conversion turn non-compliant raw EHR (Electronic Health Record) extracts into governed FHIR R4 and OMOP CDM outputs. Each layer lands as an atomic DuckLake Parquet snapshot and advances independently, so a degraded run never discards healthy data.
