# Every indexable Page states that the same person scopes and builds

The site says `wij`, and the firm is an eenmanszaak trading as DataWeGo. Checked
against the copy rather than assumed: the word "team" appears three times and each
time means the **client's** people, and the engagement sentence already promises
`geen wisseling halverwege het project`. So the plural pronoun implies no headcount.
What it does leave room for is a buyer's surprise — the footer's KvK number resolves
to one registered adviser in a single lookup, on the one point where ADR 0008's
anonymity and Q1's Dutch market necessarily meet.

Three shapes were on the table: say nothing and rely on the existing hint; rewrite
twenty pronouns to `ik`; or state the fact once as a standing clause. We chose the
clause: **`DataWeGo is een onafhankelijke data- en AI-adviseur: wie de scope bepaalt,
bouwt het ook.`**, carried by every Indexable surface in both Language variants. It
turns `onafhankelijk` into a position worth competing for instead of a gap to be
discovered, costs no rewrite of voice, and does not reopen the naming decision.

It lives in the generator's shared-clause table (ADR 0003) rather than loose in
body copy, which is the one thing generation genuinely buys here: five Pages × two
variants cannot forget a row of it, and a hand-kept site would.

The clause is a claim that has to stay true. Taking on a partner or subcontracting
delivery means editing it in ten places at once — which is the point of keeping it
in one table.
