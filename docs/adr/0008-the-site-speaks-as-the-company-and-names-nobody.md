# The site speaks as the company and names nobody

Every Page speaks as `we` / `wij`, and no person is named anywhere in the 1,462
words of copy. The alternative put to the owner was a byline per Practice-area
page — one real name, their LinkedIn, and a JSON-LD `Person` hanging off it — on
the grounds that healthcare queries sit in Google's YMYL band, where *Experience*
is nearly impossible to establish through an anonymous corporate first person, and
because `#approach` already promises that the architect who scopes an engagement
is the engineer who builds it.

The owner declined. The site stays anonymous, and the promise about who does the
work stays as a statement of method rather than an attribution to a person.

What that costs, recorded so nobody has to rediscover it: no author or speaker
attribution is available to Google, which removes one of the few machine-checkable
expertise signals a consultancy can emit; a Dutch healthcare buyer who wants to
know who they would actually be working with cannot find out on the site and has
to ask in the enquiry; and `#approach`'s continuity claim is now unverifiable by
construction.

The mitigation this decision chooses instead is company-level authority: the
signals a site can emit without naming anyone — KvK registration, an office in Den
Haag, the company's LinkedIn as `sameAs`, and register-verifiable facts about the
organisation — carried in structured data built from one source rather than
restated in prose. The trade is deliberate: the firm's accountability stays with
the firm.
