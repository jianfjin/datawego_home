# No Google Business Profile

The recommendation was to open one, address hidden behind a service-area profile,
on the grounds that a brand query would otherwise be answered by someone else. The
owner declined: verification needs a real address, and once reviews are open the
firm cannot decide what appears beside its own name.

Consequences accepted here, because they are real:

- `datawego` plus `Den Haag`, and the map/knowledge panel beside a brand search,
  are composed by third parties — the KvK aggregators, Clutch, LinkedIn — rather
  than by the company.
- With no Profile, the KvK number, the Den Haag location and `info@datawego.nl`
  become the only facts Google can cross-check the site against. Consistency
  between the footer, the structured data and the register stops being tidiness
  and becomes the mechanism: a site that states the company differently in two
  places has no other authority to fall back on.
- The structured data in ADR 0011 is therefore the firm's only submission channel
  to Google about itself, which is why it is generated from one source rather than
  typed into a dashboard.
